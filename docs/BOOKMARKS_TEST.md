# Bookmarks, clips and notes — Windows test checklist

1. Open an owned book and play past 30 seconds. In the mini player, **◇** saves a bookmark, **✂** captures the preceding 30 seconds (less near the start), and **☷** returns to the saved-items panel in the library. Playback should continue during capture and when returning to the library. Ctrl+Alt+B should still save a bookmark.
2. In **Bookmarks, clips & notes**, check the book selector, Everything/Bookmarks/Clips/Notes filters, search and sorting. Reading saved items must not start playback. Older notes and bookmarks should retain their IDs, text and timestamps; older items may have no saved date.
3. Choose **Clip last 30 seconds**. The times should stay fixed while typing. Adjust Start/End with `hours:minutes:seconds`, or use Start here/End here. Save with and without a note. Invalid times, an end before the start, ranges beyond the book, and clips longer than five minutes should be rejected.
4. Create a note, switch books before saving, then switch back: its draft must remain associated with the original book. Closing or signing out clears unsaved drafts. Saving an item should not clear a different book's draft.
5. Choose **Play clip** on the current open book. Verify audio begins at the saved start and pauses at its end. End timing is best effort, driven by the official media element. **Return to listening** seeks back to the position captured before the first clip replay. It does not automatically resume a paused player. Replay moves the real timeline and may update Audible's listening position.
6. Seek or change chapters while a clip is playing. The previous clip's end must no longer stop or move playback. Normal Play after a finished clip must continue normally. Try replay while History is asking you to choose a position; the clip must be blocked until you choose.
7. Edit text and start/end times, then read the item again. In two views or queued requests, an old edit must not overwrite a newer one. Delete should ask for confirmation; cancellation must preserve it. Old unassigned notes remain available through the explicit old-notes export.
8. Export with EXPORT NOTES. The JSON should include kind, position, end_position and optional creation time. It contains no audio or credentials, but does contain readable notes and book IDs. Cancel once; no file should be created. Keep exports private.
9. Sign out during load/save/replay, then use a different account. No saved items, drafts or pending commands from the first account should appear in or modify the second. Reopening the original account should restore its saved items.
10. Test narrow/large windows, light and dark themes, keyboard navigation and Windows display scaling. The mini-player capture buttons should remain visible at its minimum width.

Saved items are local and encrypted with the account's existing database. They do not import or sync Audible mobile bookmarks/clips. A clip is a start/end range replayed through the signed-in official player, not a copied or downloadable audio file. The app must be connected and that title already open for replay. Bookmarks from older versions are identified by their original Bookmark text; existing content is not rewritten.
